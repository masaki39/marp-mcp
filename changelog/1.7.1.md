## What's New in 1.7.1

### Bug Fix: Agenda Icons No Longer Depend on icongr.am

`generate_agenda` previously loaded its numbered circle icons from icongr.am, which stopped responding, leaving agenda and section slides with broken images. The icons (Material Design Icons `numeric-1-circle` – `numeric-9-circle`, Apache-2.0) are now bundled in the repository under `assets/icons/` and referenced via:

```
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/masaki39/marp-mcp/main/assets/icons/numeric-{N}-circle-{color}.svg
```

Two colors are provided: `666666` (agenda list) and `ffffff` (section slides).
